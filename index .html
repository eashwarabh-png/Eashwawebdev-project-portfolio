Practical Work

Two ways in which I have tried to help people around me.

Case Study | Income Support

Helping a College Chef Earn More

The Situation: A chef who works in a college is the only earning member of his family of five, which includes three children. His finances were very tight.

What I Did: I helped him draft a letter asking for a raise in salary. It has been approved and will come into effect by the end of the year. Until then, I helped him plan how to take a few catering orders in his free time, as he is a very talented cook but did not have the ideas to reach more clients. First we worked on his grooming and on a few communication skills that were missing. Then we sent food samples to some people who could recommend him to others, along with his business cards, which I helped to print.

What Came of It: We received calls for two birthday parties and for a housewarming ceremony with three catering events. The guests enjoyed the food, and this was written in the feedback form. In about one and a half months he earned almost Rs. 8,000 to Rs. 10,000 extra, and he now has two more orders waiting.
